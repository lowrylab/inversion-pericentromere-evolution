# Mechanistic recombination/purging experiment design

## Decision

Retain the completed perfectly coupled maintenance baseline as arm A and add a variation-enabled arm B. Arm A remains the existing 700-run result and is not modified. Arm B introduces neutral within-orientation phase variation using two neutral markers per orientation, preserving the adaptive fitness model, mutation model, DFE, demographic parameters, and validated recombination callback.

The new model is [mechanistic_within_orientation_variation.slim](<audit workspace>/corrected_maintenance_baseline_2026-09-26/models/mechanistic_within_orientation_variation.slim), SHA256 `71124f228b292f5324b054540e45d6cf07d15ce0c316909c68a543574783ca70`. No production replicates have been launched.

## How variation is introduced

Within every population, half of the initial A chromosomes receive neutral markers m7 at 350 kb and m8 at 650 kb; the other half receive neither. Thus A starts with two parental phases, 11 and 00. Half of B chromosomes receive neutral markers m9 and m10 at the same positions; the other half receive neither. B therefore has its own parental 11 and 00 phases.

The marker mutations have zero fitness effect, are not part of the deleterious DFE, and do not alter the adaptive markers m2/m3 or m5/m6. They only provide ancestry/phase labels. A/A and B/B recombination can create 10 and 01 recombinant phases. A/B recombination remains completely suppressed in the inversion treatment, and all recombination remains suppressed in the permanent comparator.

This is cleaner than seeding different deleterious backgrounds because it does not change initial load, selection, dominance, or the number of deleterious mutations. Burden remains generated by the same recurrent m4 process, so a burden shift can be analyzed conditional on neutral ancestry rather than being built into the initial load contrast.

## Required treatments

Use the same N=1000, S=0.05, mutation rate, DFE, dominance, focal map, initial orientation frequency, and 10,000-generation framework as the completed baseline. Use m=0, 0.01, and 0.05, with matched seeds between inversion and permanent suppression. The baseline arm A should be retained as a reference, but production arm B should be run separately with its own manifest and model hash.

## Diagnostics

At every logging interval the model records A/B frequency, genotype state, parental and recombinant 11/00/10/01 phases within A and B, orientation–adaptive LD, adaptive–neutral-marker LD, focal and chromosome-2 burden, and A/B conditional burden distributions with mean, median, q90, mutation-copy totals, and effect-weighted totals. `FIXED` records preserve the fixed-m4 substitution accounting used in the baseline.

## Mechanistic predictions

Evidence for recombination-mediated uncoupling would be: nonzero A or B recombinant phases in the inversion arm, declining adaptive–neutral-marker LD or burden–marker LD specifically in A/A or B/B occupancy, and lower burden among surviving A/B-background haplotypes without a corresponding increase in loss of the whole A class. The permanent arm should retain parental phase and high LD except for drift.

Evidence for selective loss rather than purging would be: recombinant phases remain absent or irrelevant, A frequency falls, the conditional burden distribution is truncated because high-load A haplotypes disappear, and population-wide burden falls mainly through reduced A occupancy. The key comparison is therefore within surviving neutral-background classes, not only the overall A mean.

## Smoke-test result

The corrected model compiles under SLiM 5.1. In a short N=1000, 1000-generation test at m=0.01, the inversion arm produced nonzero within-A and within-B recombinant phase frequencies, while the permanent-suppression arm produced zero recombinant phases. The callback therefore remains consistent with the required A/A, A/B, B/B rule. A first initialization attempt was corrected because an Eidos loop iterated individual haplosomes rather than the intended haplosome sets; the corrected initialization was retested before this report.

## Production gate

Before launching arm B, confirm the following in a final short smoke test: initial A and B marker-phase frequencies are approximately 0.25 for 11 and 0.25 for 00 within each orientation; inversion recombinant phases become nonzero; permanent recombinant phases remain zero; m2/m3 and m5/m6 remain adaptive markers rather than neutral marker surrogates; and burden accounting remains internally consistent. No selection × migration grid or rare-invasion experiment should be started.
